the instantaneous physical jump — **a proxy, not an identity.** Un-damping the 30d-averaged implied-variance
jump over a plausible vol mean-reversion (κ 10–40/yr; damping factor 0.29–0.68):

So the observed μ_v is **data-anchored to ≈0.12–0.39 (central ~0.17)** — the co-jump is **real and somewhat
larger** than N1 assumed. **ρ_J (sign/strength) is decision-grade; the μ_v MAGNITUDE is data-anchored but
carries the implied→instantaneous mapping range** (that residual is the honest caveat).
